About Us

Our Journey Since 2006

Sri Prabhudeva Yoga Kendra was established in 2006 with the vision of "Yoga for Health". What started as a small group of dedicated yoga practitioners has grown into a strong community wellness initiative.

We provide free daily yoga sessions focusing on physical fitness, mental peace and healthy living. Our programs mainly support middle-aged adults, senior citizens and women.

Along with yoga practice, we conduct health awareness programs, community service activities, cultural events and spiritual tours to promote harmony and wellbeing in society.
